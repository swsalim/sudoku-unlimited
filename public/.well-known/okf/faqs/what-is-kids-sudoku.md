# What is kids Sudoku?

Kids Sudoku is a smaller Sudoku grid. 4×4 uses digits 1–4 and 2×2 boxes. 6×6 uses digits 1–6 and boxes of 2 rows by 3 columns. Each digit appears once in every row, column, and box. Both sizes can be played in the browser and printed with a solution page.

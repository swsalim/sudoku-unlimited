# Business Context

- **Name:** Sudoku Unlimited
- **Type:** ONLINE-only (free web puzzle game; no signup required)
- **Audience:** Casual puzzle players who want free browser Sudoku — beginners through advanced — plus people looking for a daily challenge, a Sudoku solver, or Killer Sudoku variants. Global English-speaking audience.
- **Industry:** Online games / puzzle / casual entertainment
- **Location:** None (online-only; no physical locations)
- **Description:** Free in-browser Sudoku puzzles from Easy to Extreme, plus Daily Challenge, Sudoku Solver, and Killer Sudoku. No download or account required. Competes with large established Sudoku sites on a young domain (registered Feb 2025).

# Writing Instructions

- Clear, friendly how-to tone. Lead with playability and free access.
- Prefer concrete difficulty and feature names already on the site (Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, Master, Extreme, Killer, Daily, Solver).
- Do not invent paid plans, apps, or downloads.

# Topics to Avoid

- Paid Sudoku apps or in-app purchases (this site is free)
- Gambling / lottery framing
- Medical or cognitive-health claims

# Content Tone

Friendly, clear, instructional — like a patient puzzle teacher. professional-enough for guides, but not corporate.
